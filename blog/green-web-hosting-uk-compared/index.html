What Counts as “Green” Hosting?

The term gets abused. Genuine green hosting means one of three things, ideally two:

  1. Renewable-powered data centres - running on matched or directly-contracted renewable energy (solar, wind, hydro). Verified by a third party.
  2. Carbon-matched operations - not just the servers but cooling, offices, networking, procurement.
  3. Listed on The Green Web Foundation - an independent, auditable registry. This is the check we run before recommending any host.

“Carbon neutral” on its own is usually a tell. It often means “we buy offsets for diesel backup power” rather than “we do not burn diesel in the first place”. Dig into the methodology before trusting the marketing - we break the two labels down in carbon neutral vs green web hosting.

What to Watch Out For

  • “Green” badges without disclosure. If a host claims green credentials but does not publish an annual sustainability report, treat the claim as marketing. Genuine providers publish detail.
  • Offsets as the sole mechanism. Offsets are a last-resort mitigation, not a substitute for reduction. Providers who lean heavily on “carbon neutral” language usually mean “we buy offsets”.
  • Unlimited bandwidth on £2/month plans. Uneconomic hosting is usually shared on overloaded servers. Performance suffers, meaning your pages load slowly, meaning users wait longer, meaning more energy consumed. A £8/month plan that actually performs is greener than a £2/month plan that does not.
  • “Premium” tiers that are basically the same product. Some providers charge a premium for “green” hosting when their infrastructure is already green at the base tier. Do not pay twice.

Common questions

What is green web hosting?

Green web hosting is web hosting infrastructure powered by verified renewable energy - typically wind, solar, or hydro - confirmed by an independent registry such as The Green Web Foundation. The most credible providers go further by purchasing direct Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) rather than relying solely on Renewable Energy Certificates.

Is green web hosting available in the UK?

Yes. Several UK-based providers run on verified renewable energy, including Krystal (UK data centres), Kualo, and 34SP.com. Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, and Vercel also run on renewable-powered infrastructure with global edge networks that include UK points of presence. We list current pricing, performance, and caveats for each in the comparison above.

How much more does green web hosting cost in the UK?

Green web hosting in the UK is typically priced the same as conventional hosting at the entry tier. Krystal's shared hosting starts at the same price points as the major non-green incumbents, and Cloudflare Pages and Netlify offer free tiers on green infrastructure. Premium tiers may sit 10-20% higher than the cheapest grey alternatives, but the gap is much smaller than most people assume.

How can I check if my current host is green?

Run your domain through The Green Web Foundation's public checker at thegreenwebfoundation.org/green-web-check. It returns an immediate verified or not-verified result and lists which renewable claims the host has registered. This is the same check we run on every site we build or audit.

Will switching to green hosting reduce my website's carbon emissions?

Switching the host alone reduces the operational emissions of serving your site. The combined effect of green hosting plus a leaner, static-first website build is usually a 10× to 40× reduction in carbon per page view. The hosting switch is the largest single lever, but it works best as part of a broader sustainable web design approach - see our practical guide for the full picture.

Is green hosting worth it for a small UK business?

Yes, in two ways. Operationally, the price gap is now negligible, so there is no commercial reason to stay on grey infrastructure. Strategically, it is one of the few sustainability claims a small business can make that is independently verifiable in thirty seconds via a public registry - which makes it credible to customers, useful for B Corp and SME Climate Hub reporting, and stands up to scrutiny in a way that vague "eco-friendly" claims do not.

What is the best green web hosting in the UK?

There is no single best - it depends on what you are hosting. For a static or Jamstack marketing site, Cloudflare Pages is our top pick: free, fastest, and renewable-matched with 30+ UK edge locations. For a Next.js or React application, Vercel pinned to its London region. For WordPress or sites that need UK data residency and cPanel, Krystal (Manchester and London data centres, B Corp) or Kualo (London, solar-powered) are the strongest verified-green options. All four are listed on The Green Web Foundation.

Can I get green web hosting with cPanel in the UK?

Yes. Krystal (Manchester and London data centres, B Corp) and Kualo (London, solar-powered) both offer traditional cPanel shared hosting on infrastructure verified by The Green Web Foundation - from £5 and £2.99 a month respectively. OakFox's managed green hosting also runs on cPanel underneath, from £9.99 a month, with the panel available if you want it and everything handled for you if you don't.

Is carbon neutral web hosting the same as green hosting?

Not necessarily. "Carbon neutral" often means a host buys offsets to cancel out emissions it still produces - for example offsetting diesel backup generators rather than not burning diesel at all. Genuine green hosting runs on verified renewable energy in the first place, ideally via direct Power Purchase Agreements rather than certificates, and publishes an annual methodology. When you see "carbon neutral" as the headline claim, read how it is achieved before trusting it.
