Research, not borrowed credentials

Our environmental consultancy is grounded in original work. We draw on it directly when advising clients on sustainability claims, supply chains and reporting.

  • Public perception of ancient woodlands

    Original field and survey research into how UK communities understand, value and engage with ancient woodland habitats.

  • Biodiversity and UK ecosystem health

    Ongoing study of biodiversity indicators across UK ecosystems, focused on how independent businesses and landowners can make a real contribution.

  • Sustainability in practice

    Applied research into supply chain transparency, greenwashing patterns and what low-impact business looks like day to day.

What the work is built on

  1. Sustainability first, not last

    Every decision, from the suppliers we recommend to the hosting we deploy on, is weighed for its environmental impact. We work on verified renewable-powered infrastructure, and we'll tell you when a project can't be delivered responsibly on the terms proposed.

  2. Craft over volume

    We take on a small number of projects each quarter. No templates, no reskins, and no pipeline of junior designers producing work under a senior name. The person who quoted you does the work.

  3. Honest partnership

    Clear timelines, clear pricing and a clear view of what we can and can't do. If something is outside our skills, we'll say so. If a supplier you've been recommended is greenwashing, we'll say that too.

Based in West Lancashire, working UK-wide

The studio is at Martland Mill in Burscough, between Ormskirk and Southport, a few minutes from the West Lancashire coast and roughly 45 minutes from central Manchester or Liverpool. Most collaboration happens remotely, with visits in person for brand discovery, photography or installations when they matter.

We work with independent businesses and established brands across the UK, Ireland and mainland Europe. Local clients have included studios in Ormskirk, Southport, Preston and Manchester; remote clients have ranged from the Lake District and Dublin to London and further afield.
