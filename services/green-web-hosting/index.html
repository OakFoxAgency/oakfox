Before you sign up

Still deciding which kind of host you need? Our green web hosting comparison covers the main UK providers, and carbon neutral vs green hosting explains why the two labels aren't the same thing.
What is included in "managed"?

Setup, DNS configuration, SSL certificates, automated backups, security updates, uptime monitoring, and a real human to email when something is wrong. You never need to log into cPanel unless you want to.

Do you handle the migration from my current host?

Yes, free on every plan. We move your files, databases, email accounts, and DNS records, then schedule the DNS cutover for a quiet time so visitors see zero downtime. Most migrations complete inside a working day.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes. Pay yearly and you get two months free: Starter is £99.90 a year, Growth £229.90 and Business £459.90. No VAT to add.

What happens if I want to leave?

You own your data. We hand over a complete export (files, databases, DNS records) and assist with migration to your new host. No lock-in, no exit fees, no awkward conversations.

Will moving to green hosting hurt my SEO or speed?

No. Modern green hosting performs identically to standard hosting on every metric: uptime, response time, Core Web Vitals, Lighthouse score. If anything, a move from a slow shared host to managed green hosting often improves performance.

Where are your servers located?

All hosting plans run in UK data centres, which matters for GDPR, for performance to UK visitors, and for the carbon intensity of the underlying grid. Every plan is available anywhere in the UK, and we look after clients from our home patch of West Lancashire to London.

How can I check your hosting is really green?

Look it up yourself. Every site we host shows as green in The Green Web Foundation's public directory, which lists hosts running on verified renewable energy, not ones that buy offsets and call themselves neutral. Our free website carbon audit runs the same check on any site, including yours as it is today.

Why not go direct to Krystal or Kualo?

Going direct to a host is cheaper if you are comfortable managing your own hosting. OakFox's plans cost a little more because they include hands-on UK support, free migrations, ongoing maintenance, and the same person to talk to whether the question is about hosting, your site, or your brand.
