How a web design project runs

  1. Listen

    A proper conversation about what the site needs to do for the business, its visitors and the people who'll look after it.

  2. Architect

    Sitemap, user journeys and page templates, agreed before anything is designed.

  3. Design

    One direction developed in detail, rather than three shallow options, with the reasoning behind each decision.

  4. Refine

    Two rounds of revisions, tested on real content, real screens and the accessibility standard you need to meet.

  5. Hand over

    The design system, assets and a walk-through with your developers, or straight into our build if we're developing it too.

How much does a website design project cost?

Web design at OakFox typically ranges from £3,500 for a focused landing page to £15,000 or more for a full multi-page site with a design system, research and accessibility auditing. Every quote is worked out case by case, and we send a detailed written proposal after a free first call.

How long does it take to design a website?

A focused marketing site usually takes four to eight weeks from kick-off to design sign-off. A larger, content-led site with research, a design system and several rounds of stakeholder review runs ten to fourteen weeks. We agree the timeline while scoping and protect it with weekly milestone reviews.

What does "sustainable web design" actually mean?

It means reducing the energy needed to serve and use a website: lighter pages, fewer requests, efficient images, minimal JavaScript, clean semantic markup, and hosting on verified renewable-powered infrastructure. It also means designing for longevity, so the site doesn't need rebuilding in two years because a framework fell out of fashion.

Do you design accessible websites?

Yes. Accessibility is part of the design from the start rather than retrofitted. We design to WCAG 2.2 AA as a minimum: colour contrast, focus states, semantic structure, keyboard navigation, screen reader labels and testing with real content. We check accessibility at every stage, not just at the end. For a site that already exists, we also offer accessibility audits and fixes, using our own checker, A11yMate.

Do you hand over a design system or just final files?

A design system is the default: components, tokens, type scales, spacing rules and usage guidelines in a working Figma file, so future pages are quicker to build and stay consistent. Static PDFs are available if you need them, but they go out of date quickly and we don't recommend them.

Can OakFox build the website too, or do I need a separate developer?

Either works. Many clients take design and development together with us, which means fewer handoffs and faster delivery. If you already have a developer, we hand over a thorough package so they can build from the system with confidence. We also do design-only work for agencies.
