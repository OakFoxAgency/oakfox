What you get

A whole-site audit, tools and people

We scan the site with A11yMate, our own accessibility checker, then test by hand against WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard only, a screen reader, zoom and reflow, and colour and contrast. Tools catch the quick wins; people catch what stops someone finishing a purchase or a form.

Every problem in plain English, ranked by how much it hurts real people and how easy it is to fix, with the page, the cause and the fix. Written so a manager can decide and a developer can build from it.

The fixes, made in the site

We do the work, not just the report: in your WordPress theme, plugins and content. Then an accessibility statement that says what works, what does not yet and how to get help.

How an accessibility project runs

  1. Free check

    Start with the free one-page check from A11yMate. It takes a minute, needs no signup and shows what an automated scan finds on a single page.

  2. Audit

    A whole-site audit: A11yMate across every template, then manual testing by keyboard, with a screen reader, at 200% and 400% zoom and in different colour settings.

  3. Report

    A prioritised, plain-English report: what is wrong, who it affects, how to fix it and what to do first.

  4. Fix

    We make the fixes in your WordPress site, and tell you about anything that needs a decision from you, such as brand colours.

  5. Statement

    An accessibility statement written for your site: standard met, known gaps, a contact route, and the enforcement procedure that applies to you.

  6. Re-test

    We test the fixes again and report what is resolved and what remains. Nothing is signed off on the strength of a scan alone.

Accessibility questions we hear a lot

Want it fixed at a fixed price? See the fix sprint, from £750. The fixes start in the build: see how we handle accessible web design and development.
What does an accessibility audit cost?

It depends on the size of the site and how many different page types it has, so we quote a fixed price once we have seen it. The free one-page check is the starting point: send us the result, or just the site address, and we reply within 24 hours with a fixed quote for the audit, and for the fixes if you want them done. If you mainly want the worst problems fixed on a small site, a fix sprint does that in a week, from £750.

Do you guarantee compliance?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. Accessibility is about real people being able to use your site, not a badge. We test against WCAG 2.2 AA and fix what we find, and we tell you plainly what is left. Legal compliance is for a court or regulator to decide, and we are not lawyers.

Do you install an accessibility overlay or widget?

No. Overlays are a script that sits on top of a site and claims to fix it. They do not fix the underlying code, they can get in the way of people who already use assistive technology, and they have not protected sites from complaints. We fix the site itself.

Why not just run an automated scanner?

Automated tools, including ours, find only part of the problems. They cannot tell whether alt text is useful, whether a focus order makes sense or whether a form can be completed with a screen reader. That is why people test too.

What is the European Accessibility Act?

An EU law that has applied since 28 June 2025. It requires many consumer-facing products and services, including e-commerce, banking, e-books and passenger transport, to be accessible. Micro-enterprises providing services (fewer than 10 staff and turnover or balance sheet under €2 million) are exempt for services, but their customers and larger clients may still ask. Check how it applies to you.

Does the Equality Act apply to my website?

In the UK, the Equality Act 2010 requires service providers to make reasonable adjustments for disabled people, and that covers online services. There is no single legal standard for websites, but WCAG 2.2 AA is the benchmark that is widely used and referred to.

Can you fix sites that are not on WordPress?

Mostly we fix WordPress sites, where A11yMate runs, and hand-built sites we can edit directly. On hosted builders such as Wix, Squarespace or Shopify some fixes are out of anyone's reach, and we tell you which before you pay. Where a theme or plugin limits what can be fixed, we say so in the report.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site and what we find. We give you the timeline with the quote, before you commit.
