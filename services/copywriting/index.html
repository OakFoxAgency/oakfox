How a copywriting project runs

  1. Listen

    A conversation about what the business does, who it talks to and what's been written before. We read your existing copy, your reviews, and what customers have said to you in their own words.

  2. Scope

    A one-page brief covering pages, word counts, voice and deadlines, agreed before writing starts so nobody's guessing at the end.

  3. Draft

    A first pass written to the brief: opinionated enough to react to, restrained enough to be a real draft rather than a showreel.

  4. Refine

    Two rounds of edits as standard. We push back where we disagree, and defer where the call is yours.

  5. Hand over

    Copy delivered as a Google Doc, ready for your CMS, or straight into the page, plus voice notes if they'll help whoever writes next.

Copywriting questions we hear a lot

Why vague green claims cost more than they win: how to write sustainability claims that aren't greenwashing.
How much does website copywriting cost?

Copywriting projects at OakFox typically range from £900 for a single, carefully written landing page to £6,000 or more for a full website copy overhaul including voice guidelines. Every quote is priced on scope and expertise rather than word count, and we send a detailed proposal after a free first call.

Do you write SEO-optimised copy?

Yes, but we write for readers first and search engines second. Keyword research shapes the topic and structure, not the sentences. Pages rank because they answer a search better than the competition, not because the target phrase is in every paragraph. The copy we deliver reads naturally and ranks.

What is the difference between copywriting and content writing?

Copywriting is persuasive, commercial writing: homepages, service pages, email sequences and landing pages. Content writing is editorial or educational: articles, guides and long-form resources. We do both, but brief them differently. Copy earns a decision; content earns trust.

Will you use my existing brand voice or create one?

Either. If you have a voice guide, we stress-test it and write to it. If you don't, we can develop one as the first part of the project: principles, tone rules, vocabulary lists, and examples of good and bad for your team to refer to. It takes a few hours and keeps paying off.

How do you handle sustainability claims and avoid greenwashing?

Every claim we write is one you could defend to a regulator. We ask for evidence, check supply chains where relevant, and won't write vague language like "eco-friendly" or "planet-positive" without substantiation. Clear claims and honest caveats make a stronger brand; greenwashing is a short-term gain and a long-term reputational risk.

How many rounds of revisions are included?

Two rounds of edits are included as standard on all copywriting work, and most projects never need the second. Extra rounds are charged at a transparent hourly rate agreed in the proposal, but they're rarely needed.
